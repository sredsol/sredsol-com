What I would refine now

I would make four very small, high-impact refinements, not another redesign.

1. Strengthen the visual link between the four pillars and the five/seven-stage progression

Currently they are conceptually related, but visually they're separate sections.

I would introduce a subtle common motif.

For example:

COMPUTATION
       │
PHYSICAL SYSTEMS
       │
OBSERVATION
       │
LEARNING

and let the later progression reuse the same tiny node/connector language.

Not a literal connector between sections.

Just a shared visual grammar.

This will make the entire page feel like one system.
